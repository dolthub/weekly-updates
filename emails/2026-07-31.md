### One Week From Doltgres 1.0

One our company advisors called the coming Doltgres 1.0 launch "DoltHub's biggest launch ever". I balked. 

But the more I thought about it, the more I became convinced. Postgres is the dominant database format of the 2020s. Finally having production database version control in the most popular SQL dialect just as agents start to require it just might be the right product at the right time. 

[Jason](https://www.dolthub.com/team#jason) wrote [an article about the progress we've made](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-07-30-doltgres-1-0-one-week-out/) over the past month in preparation for next week's announcement.

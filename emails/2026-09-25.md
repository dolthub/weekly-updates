### Replication Week

It's replication week here at DoltHub! [Dumbo](https://github.com/dolthub/dumbodb), the MongoDB-compatible version of Dolt, now can [act as a replica of your MongoDB](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-09-22-dumbodb-replication). [MongoDB insurance](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-05-04-database-insurance/), anyone?

We also [launched wildcard binlog filtering](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-09-23-wildcard-binlog-filters) for original Dolt per a user request. This feature allows you to make Dolt a partial replica of your running MySQL, versioning only the most important tables.

### SQL Test Labs

Every once in a while, we wake up to a swarm of bugs in [our GitHub issues](https://github.com/dolthub/dolt/issues). As many of you know, we try to [fix bugs in 24 hours or less](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2024-05-15-24-hour-bug-fixes/). So, when we see a swarm of issues, we get a little nervous. Sometimes, these bug swarms are filed by SQL tests labs, like the one at the National University of Singapore (NUS). Knowing these are researcher bugs and not blocking customers is comforting because it lets us fix the bugs at a more leisurely pace. 

[Angela](https://www.dolthub.com/team#angela) is the person on our team who fixes SQL analyzer bugs, where researcher issues tend to land. She wrote [about the NUS TEST lab](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-09-18-nus-test-lab/) as the introduction to a series of articles on what these SQL test labs are up to.  

### Supadolt?

[Supabase](https://github.com/supabase/supabase) is a popular open source database platform. I had always thought of it as "RedHat for Postgres", packaging up and maintaining best-of-breed Postgres tools into an easy-to-use package. It is that, but a Dolt user described it to me as a "backend in a box" claiming the authorization features built around Postgres row-level security removed the need for a web service in front of your Postgres. 

Intrigued, I sent [Dustin](https://www.dolthub.com/team#dustin) off to explore Supabase. He wrote a great [in depth guide](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-09-21-a-tour-of-supabase/) about what it is and how to use it, complete with an example. Should we do something similar with Dolt or Doltgres? Supadolt? Reply to this email if you have opinions.

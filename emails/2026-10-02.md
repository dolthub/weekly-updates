### Branches vs Clones

Continuing with the snarky theme this week, I've long said [a fork is not a branch](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2024-09-18-database-branches/). Databases claiming to have branches really just have forks. We recently learned that Postgres 18 added a facility to fast copy, the utility used by [Neon](https://neon.com/) and others, to provide "database branches" or as I like to call them "forks". I forced [Aaron](https://www.dolthub.com/team#aaron) to write about [how this new Postgres facility works](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-10-01-postgres-cheap-database-clones/) and like [Nick](https://www.dolthub.com/team#nick), he is far more gracious to the competition than I would have been.

Dolt is the only database with branches.

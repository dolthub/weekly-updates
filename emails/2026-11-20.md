### DoltLite 100

It used to be hard to put data in Dolt. Now it's a prompt. I wanted sample DoltLite databases on DoltHub for people to try. In an afternoon, [I had 100](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-11-18-100-doltlites). When we started this company, this was a year of work. What a time to be alive.

### LLM SQL Benchmarks

How good are LLMs at SQL? Which models and harnesses are best? Being a SQL database targeting agent use, we were curious. [Eric](https://www.dolthub.com/team#eric) [surveyed the latest research in the field](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-11-19-agentic-sql-benchmarks/). Current benchmarks leave more questions than answers. We'd love more research in this space.

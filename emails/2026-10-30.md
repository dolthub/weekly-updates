### Datalib

A new project from [Imbue](https://imbue.com/) called [datalib uses DoltLite](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-10-29-doltlite-datalib/)! I tried it out and made some recommendations about how to extend your datalib use to other Dolt products.

### Jev

We might be a little late to the party, but we [reviewed Jev this week](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-11-12-working-with-jev). Jev is a new type of model that makes decisions: a general purpose classifier, similar to traditional machine learning models. 

We had a cool internal use case and wanted to try Jev on it. Jev works! [Dustin](https://www.dolthub.com/team#dustin) dives into [what Jev is and how to use it](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-11-12-working-with-jev) while walking you through our example use case. I really enjoyed this one. I learned a lot.

### Lazy Flush

Another big Dolt performance win just dropped. It's called [Lazy Flush](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-11-13-lazy-flush/). It improved Dolt performance on the TPC-C benchmark by about 25%. We're still a bit slower than MySQL on that benchmark but we're up to 25% faster than MySQL on `sysbench`. Dolt will continue to get faster. 

### Another Dumbo?

There's another version-controlled MongoDB offering called [Argon](https://github.com/argon-lab/argon). It takes a different version control approach than Dumbo, or any of the Dolt family of version-controlled databases. [Neil](https://ww.dolthub.com/team#neil) gives it [a thorough review](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-11-10-argon-spotlight) including performance tests of various core Mongo and version control functionality.

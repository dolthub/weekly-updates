### DoltLite on DoltHub

Most of you know that [Dolt now comes in four flavors](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-07-16-dolt-in-4-flavors/): Dolt, Doltgres, DoltLite, and Dumbo. We're working to make all four flavors work with all our surrounding services, including DoltHub. DoltHub is Dolt's GitHub. You push to it as a remote. Like GitHub, DoltHub is where you manage Pull Requests.

A couple weeks ago, we announced [Doltgres databases are fully supported on DoltHub](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-10-19-doltgres-on-dolthub/). Hot on the heels of that announcement, [DoltLite databases are now fully supported on DoltHub](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-11-02-doltlite-on-dolthub/) as well.

### Dumbo Improves

[Neil](https://www.dolthub.com/team#neil) keeps plugging away at [Dumbo, the MongoDB-compatible Dolt](https://github.com/dolthub/dumbodb). Almost more impressively, he ships [a blog article per week](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/?tags=dumbo) about it. He really is shouting at the internet until it listens, i.e., the DoltHub marketing strategy. This week, he announced [a slew of Mongo-compatibility improvements](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-11-03-dumbodb-operators/). 

Dumbo is getting pretty good and stable, nearing a Beta release. We're looking for Beta testers. If you have a Dumbo use case, just reply to this email.

### Cursors in Doltgres

We're slowly tackling the immense surface area of Postgres in Doltgres. Thank you all for your issues. Issue velocity has definitely increased since the [Doltgres 1.0 release](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-08-06-doltgres-1-0/). If you find something missing, please [file an issue](https://github.com/dolthub/doltgresql/issues), and we'll try and fix it in 24 hours or less. This promise is less impressive in the vibe code era, but know that Doltgres is [trad code](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-03-26-vibe-code-vs-trad-code/). Humans are still reviewing every line.

This week, we [announced cursor support](https://www.dolthub.com/blog/2026-11-04-doltgres-cursors/), an advanced way to manage query results.
